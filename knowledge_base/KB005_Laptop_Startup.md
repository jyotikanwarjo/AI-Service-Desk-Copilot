KB Name: Laptop Will Not Start

Article ID: KB005



Troubleshooting Steps:



1\. Confirm the laptop is connected to an approved power source.



2\. Check whether any power indicator is visible.



3\. Attempt to start the laptop again.



4\. Record any error message, indicator, or unusual behavior.



5\. If the laptop remains unable to start, escalate according to the approved Hardware Support process.



Escalation:

Escalate to the approved Hardware Support team when the documented steps do not resolve the issue.



Restrictions:

Do not open the laptop or perform unauthorized hardware repairs.
