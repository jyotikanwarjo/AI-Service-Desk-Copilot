KB Name: Corporate Account Locked

Article ID: KB006



Troubleshooting Steps:



1\. Confirm that the user is unable to authenticate to the corporate account.



2\. Collect the exact account-lock or authentication error message.



3\. Confirm when the problem began.



4\. Follow the approved account recovery or unlock process.



5\. If the documented recovery process does not resolve the issue, escalate to the approved Access Management team.



Escalation:

Escalate to the approved Access Management team when the approved recovery process is unsuccessful.



Restrictions:

Never request, reveal, or record the user's password, MFA code, or other authentication secrets.
