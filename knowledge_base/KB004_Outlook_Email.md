KB Name: Outlook Unable to Send Email

Article ID: KB004



Troubleshooting Steps:



1\. Confirm Outlook is open and operating normally.



2\. Check whether the affected message remains in the Outbox.



3\. Confirm the user has an active network connection.



4\. Retry sending the message.



5\. If the problem persists, collect the exact error message and timestamp.



Escalation:

Escalate to the approved Messaging Support team if the documented troubleshooting steps do not resolve the issue.



Restrictions:

Do not request passwords or authentication secrets.

Do not make unauthorized mailbox configuration changes.
