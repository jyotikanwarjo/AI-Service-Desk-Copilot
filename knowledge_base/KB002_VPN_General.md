KB Name: General VPN Connection Troubleshooting

Article ID: KB002



Troubleshooting Steps:



1\. Confirm the device has a working internet connection.

&#x20;  1.1 Verify that the user can access a normal internet resource.



2\. Confirm the approved VPN client is being used.



3\. Close and reopen the approved VPN client.



4\. Retry the VPN connection.



5\. If the problem persists, collect the exact VPN error message and timestamp.



Escalation:

Escalate to the approved VPN/Network support team when the documented steps have been exhausted.



Restrictions:

Do not request or record passwords.

Do not reinstall the VPN client without required approval.
