KB Name: VPN Not Connecting After Password Change

Article ID: KB001



Troubleshooting Steps:



1\. Confirm the user is entering their current corporate password in the approved VPN client.

&#x20;  1.1 Do not ask the user to reveal or record their password.

&#x20;  1.2 Confirm the user is using the approved VPN client.



2\. Close and reopen the approved VPN client.

&#x20;  2.1 Retry the VPN connection.



3\. Confirm the laptop has a working internet connection.



4\. Retry the VPN connection using the current corporate credentials.



5\. If the VPN still fails, collect the exact VPN error message and timestamp.



Escalation:

If the documented troubleshooting steps do not resolve the issue, escalate to the approved VPN/Network support team.



Restrictions:

Do not request, reveal, or record passwords.

Do not reinstall the VPN client without required approval.
